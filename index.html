Taste Haven — AI Voice Receptionist

Designed and built an AI-powered voice receptionist that automates appointment and reservation management for businesses. Proven end-to-end on a restaurant booking flow for a fictional client, Taste Haven — the AI answers the call, checks live calendar availability, books or declines the table in real time, and never tells a caller "confirmed" unless it actually is. The same engineering pattern adapts directly to clinics, salons, and any appointment-based business.

Core Capabilities

  • AI-powered voice conversations using Vapi
  • Real-time availability checking with Google Calendar
  • Automatic reservation creation
  • Automatic email confirmations
  • Smart handling of unavailable booking slots with alternative suggestions

Version 1.1 Improvements NEW

  • Automatic logging of every booking attempt into Google Sheets
  • Automatic hourly cleanup of expired reservations
  • End-to-end workflow automation using n8n and Google Workspace
